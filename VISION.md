# Vision: Shadow Duel

## Pitch
"We have a new game to revamp with gdada: shadw.duel, like a magic clone in browser." `owner`, 9 Nov 2026

An original two-player fantasy card duel that runs in the browser: summon creatures, cast spells, set relics, and
reduce the other player's 8,000 life to zero. It is in the same genre as the big trading card games but borrows none of
their names, rules text or art. `inferred`

## What the player feels
- First minute: "I know what I can play this turn, and I can see what the other side has." `inferred`
- First hour: every hand offers a choice between developing the board, answering the opponent's board, and burning
  the opponent out, and the cards in the deck are different enough that two duels do not feel the same. `inferred`,
  from "need more card variety" (`owner`)

## Pillars
1. One shared deck list, so there is no deck building and nothing to buy. `inferred`, from the README
2. Seven elements, each with its own identity (flame races, water holds, earth walls, wind strikes first, light
   sustains, shadow drains, neutral fills). `inferred`, from `cards.js`
3. Every card is original and illustrated, and readable at the size the board shows it. `inferred`, from the art guide
4. Online play stays deterministic: both browsers run the same rules from the same seed. `inferred`, from the README

## Verbs
Summon a creature (one per turn), cast a spell, set a relic, attack a creature or the player, end the turn. Each needs
a decision: what to spend energy on this turn, what to attack, and what to hold back.

## World and look
Dark-fantasy vector illustrations at 400 × 300, seven palettes, an engraved card back. Cards are shown in the hand, on
the board, in the inspect panel and in the collection.

## Must-haves
1. GDADA has a tool for card art. (`owner`, "need to add tool to gdada for card art", 9 Nov 2026; "Add it to gdada its
   for games", 9 Nov 2026)
2. Every card is reviewed with GDADA: art and play. (`owner`, "use gdada to review all the cards", 9 Nov 2026)
3. More card variety, expanded where the game is already good. (`owner`, "expand where it is good need more card
   variety", 9 Nov 2026)
4. Every new card has original SVG art from the existing generator, and that art passes the read-size checks the old
   art is held to. (`inferred` from Must-have 1 and pillar 3)
5. Nothing that already works breaks: rules tests, art check, deterministic seeded matches and online sync. (`inferred`
   from pillar 4)
6. **Every creature has its own ability**, not only a shared keyword: something it does when summoned, attacks, is
   destroyed, or at the start of its controller's turn. The old one-line burn and heal spells get riders, so no two
   spells do the same thing at a different size. (`owner`, "cards need lots more detail like different abilities",
   9 Nov 2026)
7. **The art is more detailed:** form shading, light, texture and particles on the subject, not flat shapes with a few
   lines. (`owner`, "Art needs work"; answered "Too simple / not detailed", 9 Nov 2026)
8. **Every card is its own scene:** the composition, setting, time of day, horizon and the subject's placement vary,
   instead of one centred subject on one landscape per element. (`owner`, answered "Looks generic / samey",
   9 Nov 2026)
9. The richer art still passes the read-size checks (sep, twins, area, kept), and abilities fit one readable line on
   the card face. (`inferred` from pillar 3 and Must-haves 6-8)

## Will not do
- Recreate a specific existing game: its card names, rules text, mana symbols, frames or art. The genre is fair game,
  and the content is original. (`inferred`, standing rule)
- Add deck building, a collection economy or packs in this run. (`inferred`, scope)

## Success tests
- `npm test` and `npm run art:check` pass, and `node scripts/bot-match.cjs --seed 1 --matches 20` finishes every match.
- `node scripts/card-stats.cjs --matches 400`: no card with 30 or more plays wins under 30% or over 70% of them.
- `python3 <gdada>/skills/gdada/scripts/card_art.py --config gdada.card-art.json`: the new cards meet `sep` >= 1.6 and
  form no twins at the smallest board window.
- Card-variety matrix: every element has a card that touches the board, and every element has a relic.

## Platform and stack
Plain browser JavaScript with no build step, Node 22 with Express and ws for lobbies, and WebRTC for duels. The card
art is generated by `scripts/build-card-art.cjs`.
