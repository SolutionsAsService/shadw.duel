# Run 2 R2 Lenses (Schell)

Verdict: PASS

**R1 requests.**
1. *Fiction drives the rule:* met. Pyre Phoenix now returns to hand, which is rebirth. Sky Sovereign rallies another wind creature, so a storm king commands the flock. Deep Sea Leviathan drags the weakest enemy down. Flame Serpent's Kill (half the victim's ATK) and Arcane Automaton's small-hand draw also pull apart the old twins.
2. *Art shows the ability:* largely met. This is the Elemental Tetrad working, because aesthetics now carries mechanics. The clearest cases are Dread Reaper mid-swing, Phoenix rising from embers, Iron Golem's raised barrier, Ashen Dragon burning a victim, Wind Falcon carrying a card, Ironclad Mercenary spilling coins (paid in LP), Ancient Titan's floating cards, Mossback Tortoise's shell and sprout, Sky Hunter's return arc and Void Hound dissolving. In most frames the subject is acting, not just standing. That is the Lens of Surprise answered on the eye's first landing point.
3. *Fewer night scenes:* partly met. Night is down to 16 of 75, but about 25 of the 40 creature frames still put a moon disc in a dark sky, so the set still leans nocturnal. Element hue now separates the families well, so this no longer reads as samey.

**Does each picture tell you what it does?** About 32 of 40 do so before you read the text. The weak ones are Sky Sovereign (a static crowned spread pose with no second attacker shown), Dawn Acolyte (a held sun shows light, not a heal on death), Lord of the Void (a portrait with no DEF draining) and Tide Serpent/Stone Guardian (readable, but generic).

**Non-blocking notes for a later pass:**
- Sky Sovereign: draw a second, smaller wind bird swooping back in at its signal.
- Swap the moon for a sun or dusk glow on light and earth cards (Radiant Knight, Celestial Guardian, Mountain Behemoth) so the night share in the creature frames matches the 16/75 claim.

Mechanical near-twins remain (Ember Whelp/Void Hound death burn; Shadow Stalker/Wandering Duelist), but each has a distinct trigger condition. That is acceptable.
