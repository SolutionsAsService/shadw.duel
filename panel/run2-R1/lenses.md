# Run 2 R1 Lenses (Schell)

Verdict: OBJECT

**Lens of Curiosity / Surprise, mechanics.** This is real progress. 40 different triggers give each card a question to ask ("what happens when it dies?"), and Sky Hunter's extra attack on a kill and Worldbreaker's board wipe are genuine surprises. But about 16 of the 40 are still a number added to ATK/DEF/LP ("+200 DEF until your next turn", "restore 200 LP"). That is the same arithmetic as before with a trigger word in front. The owner asked for *different* abilities, and a third of the cards are still variations of one ability.

**Lens of Surprise, art.** Before vs cards-a/b: the backdrops are new (moons, peaks, temple pillars, stalactites), but the evidence admits the subject silhouettes are unchanged. When you open a card your eye goes to the creature, and the creature is the same glowing outline icon as before. Night covers 27 of 40 cards, so the "new" scenes also look alike. "Samey" is reduced but not cured.

**Elemental Tetrad (aesthetics agrees with mechanics).** No piece of art shows what the card *does*. Each creature is posed standing still, never mid-ability. The fiction and the rule disagree here:
- **Pyre Phoenix**: "Death: restore 500 LP and draw". A phoenix's story is rebirth, not healing.
- **Sky Sovereign**: "Attack: restore 200 LP". A heal on a storm king. Generic, and not wind.
- **Deep Sea Leviathan**: "Death: draw 2". Nothing in the leviathan's fiction suggests drawing cards.
- **Arcane Automaton / Stone Guardian**: two Dawn "+stat for good" cards that are near twins.
- Good matches worth copying: Iron Golem (shield and Barrier), Dread Reaper (Kill: grows), Mossback Tortoise (shell on death), Lord of the Void (permanent DEF loss), Ironclad Mercenary (paid in LP).

**Buildable changes (max 3)**
1. Rewrite the three mismatches so the fiction drives the rule. Pyre Phoenix: "Death: return to your hand at the next Dawn". Sky Sovereign: "Attack: other wind creatures may attack once more". Leviathan: "Death: deal 100 damage per enemy creature" (dragged under).
2. Give each subject one action prop tied to its trigger and drawn in build-card-art: Reaper's scythe mid-swing, Phoenix ash egg, Golem shield raised, Kraken tentacle gripping prey. Then the art tells you the ability before you read it.
3. Cut night to at most 14 of 40 and give dusk/day to the large creatures, so the scenes stop reading as one palette.
