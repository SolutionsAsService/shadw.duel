# R2 Readability (Solarski)

Verdict: PASS (two advisory notes, not blocking)

**R1 requests: met.**
1. *Value rim and split.* `rim()` puts a pale `--white` outline under every subject, and the aura is brighter. In the squint column the figure now separates from the ground by value, not only by hue. The shadow family gained the most: Lord of the Void and Gloom Mite read clearly on the phone board. sep < 1.6 went from 37 to 4.
2. *Distinct base shapes.* There are no twin pairs left (8 before, 0 now). On the sheet, Stone Guardian, Iron Golem, Mountain Behemoth and Worldbreaker now have different outlines rather than different interior detail.
3. *Small subjects enlarged.* The `enlarge` table covers all seven, and area < 0.12 is now 0.

**New subjects.** Most follow the rules. Each relic has its own primitive: Tidestone is a shell, Storm Feather a blade, Sun Sigil a sun disc and War Banner a flag, so none reuses the gem. The new cards also use role-matched shapes: Turning Gale is an inverted triangle, Mossback is a dome, Pyre Phoenix a V and Tidewall a square.

**Noise flags.** On the board-390/360 squints, Divine Renewal, Scorch, Fireball, Second Wind and Turning Gale each blur to one clear mass in the upper-middle. The flags there come from the 7-unit rim stroke, which is about 0.9px at 53px, and from interior engraving. They are not a silhouette failure.

**Advisory, worth doing next time the generator is touched:**
1. Riptide, Rally Cry and Storm Feather are built from lines. In the squint, Riptide splits into three parallel bands and Rally Cry and Storm Feather fade to faint slivers. Merge Riptide into one filled crest shape. Fill the bodies of the horn and the feather with the light value so each has a mass rather than an outline.
2. Wither scatters pale petals at about the same value as the rim on its flower head, so the card has more than one focal point. Drop the petals to the mid value.
