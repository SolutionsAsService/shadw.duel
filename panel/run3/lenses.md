# Run 3 Lenses (Schell)

Verdict: PASS

**Lens of the Elemental Tetrad (aesthetics serving mechanics).** Most pictures act out their rule, not just the creature's name. Deep Sea Leviathan pulls a boat under. Lord of the Void drains the small figures. Dark Bargain shows a pierced heart paying for two cards. Arcane Insight is an eye throwing three cards. Barrier and Tidewall turn arrows aside. Undertow drags one victim into a whirlpool. Rally Cry and War Banner show a raised host. Wind Falcon drops a card, Nightblade has a card in the moon, and Radiant Knight and Battle Colossus hand power to a small follower ("next creature"). The relics carry the most system: each one beams into a silhouette of its element. Colour also follows element: flame is red-orange, water teal, and wind and light pale. I found no card whose picture contradicts its rule.

**Lens of Unification.** Lead lines, a gold arched frame, jewel colours and a rose-window disc behind every subject give one strong identity. It reads as stained glass more than woodcut, but it holds together, and on the board it sets the game apart from the dark UI around it.

**Weak spots, not objections:**
- Crystal Sentinel shows a gold spear-knight. There is no crystal and no death-legacy, so it is the loosest match.
- Death triggers (Ember Whelp, Void Hound, Mossback Tortoise, Dawn Acolyte) show the living creature, not what happens when it dies.
- Lens of Surprise: the same rose disc sits in all 75 cards. At thumbnail size (board-desktop: Crystal Sentinel, Pyre Phoenix) it drowns the subject. In future, shrink or vary it on the busiest plates.
