# Run 3 Readability (Solarski)

Verdict: OBJECT (minor). The art is a clear step up from C and works as one series. Two small fixes remain before it reads well in play.

**Step up from C:** yes. In the C art, glow in one hue sat on murky ground and the silhouettes dissolved. Now dark ink leading sits against light glass with saturated element colours, so most shapes read in value alone, which is Solarski's first test.

**Series:** consistent. All cards share the frame, the way the burst is built, the line weights, the silhouettes for secondary figures, and palettes that identify each element at a glance.

**In play:** mostly yes. On board thumbnails, though, the burst is often the brightest, highest-contrast shape, so the eye goes to the sun instead of the creature.

**Weakest cards:**
- Crystal Sentinel: at board size it reads as a sunburst, and the knight is lost.
- Shadow Stalker: dark violet on dark violet, with no step in value.
- Undertow: a thin ribbon with no mass, so it fails the 53x40 one-shape test.
- Wither: the heart, the chain and the hound make three competing focal points.
- Quick Draw: the card is small and touches the frame, and the window is mostly empty.

**Changes:**
1. Crystal Sentinel and Iron Golem: make the burst smaller, or recentre it behind the head, so the brightest pane and the subject form one focal point.
2. Shadow Stalker and Undertow: lighten the subject's light end by one value step, and give Undertow a fuller body.
