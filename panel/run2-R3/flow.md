# Run 2 R3 Flow (Csikszentmihalyi)

Verdict: PASS (seat edge on record, as in run 1)

**Challenge against skill (ch. 4).** A new player does not meet 40 abilities in one duel. One copy of 75 cards, about 20 turns: each side draws some 20 cards, about 10 of them creatures. Each ability is one line of 55 characters or fewer, in one of five fixed shapes (Summon, Attack, Kill, Death, Dawn) and a small glossary. That is one notch above run 1's vanilla bodies, and the pool keeps adding combinations as skill grows: a staircase, not overload. Cheap creatures carry the simplest texts.

**Clear goals.** The prompt line, Ready tags and green outline always name the next action.

**Immediate feedback (ch. 3).** It is met. Fireball writes its log lines, shows the "−500 LP this turn" chip, and adds "Barrier"/"Next creature +400 ATK" chips for banked effects. ATK gains carry a ▲. Triggers log their effects, and a failed ability is logged, never silent.

**Residual friction, no objection.** Field cards show a trigger chip but not the text. To learn what an enemy's Death or Dawn ability will do, you tap "i" (one action), and the first surprise is logged. That is a learning cost in the first duel, not a defect.

**Seat edge.** 66.2% / 69.6%: about one game in three is still decided by seat. It is frozen and owned by the person for the next run. I do not re-object.
