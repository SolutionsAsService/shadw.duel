# Run 2 R3 Readability (Solarski)

Verdict: PASS

**R2 request 1: spells and relics as events, not badges.** Met. The centred ring is gone from all 35. Each card now acts out its effect along a diagonal. Fireball is a comet driving at a fleeing figure. Spark is an arc jumping between two figures. Divine Renewal is a shaft of light over a fallen body. Void Collapse is ground folding inward. Aegis is a shield catching a blade, and Healing Potion is a vial tipped over a fallen figure. This is the dynamic, asymmetric line of action that Solarski asks for. Arcane Insight (a centred eye) and Crystal Heart (a gem) stay emblem-like, but each has a figure or cards on a diagonal beside it, and neither shares a filled shape with another card.

**R2 request 2: scene-lit subjects.** Partly met. The rim is now weighted toward the sun or moon (full on the facing side, 70% on the far side), and some bodies carry a soft value step on their lit side (Meteor, Healing Potion, Dawn Acolyte). Form still mostly comes from outline rather than value. I do not object: the rim is one consistent house style, and at board size it costs a player no recognition. Separation is at least 1.61 and there are no twins.

**Owner's complaints.** "Too simple": answered. Compared with before.png, every card is a scene with a story, props and secondary shapes instead of a lone figure. "Generic / samey": answered. Across creatures-1/2 and spells-1/2, no two filled silhouettes match. Element hue groups the cards, but shape and pose tell them apart.

Nothing left here costs a player anything.
